Major maize pests in Pakistan

Pakistan میں مکئی کی فصل کو سب سے زیادہ نقصان دینے والے کیڑے stem borer, armyworm, aphids, اور fall armyworm ہیں۔

PestSymptomsControl
Stem borerWilted leaves, tunneling stemsBiopesticides, pheromone traps, neem spray
ArmywormDefoliation, leaf damageSpinosad, carbaryl, early scouting
AphidsHoneydew, curled leavesInsecticidal soap, imidacloprid
Fall armywormLeaf feeding, whorl damageBt maize, foliar insecticide

Integrated pest management

Use crop rotation, natural enemies, and spot treatment to reduce pest pressure. Avoid blanket spraying to protect pollinators and beneficial insects.
